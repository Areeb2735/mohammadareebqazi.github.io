About

I'm a research engineer working on world models, video generation, and large-scale multimodal ML systems — currently building the pre-training data pipeline for a video-based world model at the Institute of Foundation Models. My background spans diffusion transformers, vision-language models, model merging, and continual learning, applied to real deployed systems ranging from healthcare imaging to production logistics AI.

I hold an MSc in Computer Vision from MBZUAI (fully funded, GPA 3.67/4) and a BSc in Computer Science from Aligarh Muslim University (CPI 8.72/10).

Research interests: World models, video generation, diffusion transformers (DiT), multimodal foundation models, large-scale data curation & filtering, continual learning, model merging, efficient fine-tuning.

Skills: PyTorch, Transformers, Diffusers, DiT, LoRA/PEFT, distributed training (FSDP, DeepSpeed), GPU cluster management, model quantization/pruning/distillation, LangChain, CrewAI, RAG pipelines, Python, C++, Docker.
